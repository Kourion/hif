HIF to JPG

Converted on this device. Nothing is uploaded, and it works without a connection once installed.

Once installed, you can also share HIF files to this app from Files or your gallery.

On a computer, you can drop files anywhere on this page.

Settings

90 keeps detail with a reasonable file size. 100 also keeps full colour resolution, but files get very large.

Size

Longest side in pixels. Smaller photos are quicker to share and use less memory.

Copies date, camera, lens and exposure settings into the JPG.

Strips GPS coordinates before you share.
